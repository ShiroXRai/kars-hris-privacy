Account Deletion — KARS HRIS

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This page explains how KARS HRIS users (employees of KARS Teknologi Asia) can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Important note about KARS HRIS accounts

KARS HRIS is an internal Human Resource Information System for KARS employees. Accounts are created and managed by the company's HR department, not by users themselves — the app does not offer self-registration. If your employment with the company has ended, or you otherwise need your account closed, follow the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an account deletion request to the company HR team at: karstechnologiesasia@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: KARS HRIS Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your full name and employee ID (NIK) so we can verify your identity.

Alternatively, contact your company's HR department through official internal channels and ask them to close your KARS HRIS account.

What happens after your request

  • Your HRIS account will be deactivated so you can no longer sign in.
  • Your personal profile data (name, contact details, photo, etc.) will be deleted from the HRIS system.
  • Data that is kept: attendance, leave, and payroll records may be retained for the period required by Indonesian labour and tax laws (generally up to 5 years), as these are legal obligations of the employer.

Processing time

Requests are processed within 30 days of verified receipt.

Questions

For any questions about this policy or your data, contact karstechnologiesasia@gmail.com.
